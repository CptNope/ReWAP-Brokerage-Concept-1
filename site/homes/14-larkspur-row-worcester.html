What to know before you offer

  • Lead paintBuilt before 1978. Massachusetts requires the seller to provide the Property Transfer Lead Paint Notification, and the buyer has a right to an inspection.
  • Smoke and COA smoke and carbon monoxide detector compliance certificate from the local fire department is required at transfer.
  • TenanciesExisting leases transfer with the building. Review each lease, security deposits and last month’s rent held before closing.
  • ZoningConfirm the legal number of units and any open permits with the Worcester building department before the purchase and sale agreement.

General guidance written by REWAP; confirm every item for a real property.
