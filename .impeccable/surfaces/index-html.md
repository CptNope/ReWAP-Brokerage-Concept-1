## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard for a top-tier brokerage, executed at Christie's International Real Estate craft: a gallery-and-catalogue world where white space, exact captions and quiet type create authority. Refuses the template brokerage (stock hero, icon-card rows, gold-on-black).

OWN-WORLD: Gallery-white walls (near-neutral, never cream), Registry Navy fields for statement rooms, bronze held to the mark and hairline rules. Libre Caslon Display for headings, Libre Caslon Text for counsel prose, Public Sans caps-tracked for labels and tabular data (amended during build: Libre Franklin ships no tabular figures; Public Sans is drawn from Libre Franklin and adds them, which property data requires). Every image and property carries a museum-style wall caption. Square corners, 1px rules, no shadows beyond one soft lift.

STORY: The reader understands REWAP as an attorney-founded advisory brokerage, believes the system is complete and buildable, and leaves with downloadable marks, tokens and component rules.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left two-thirds: the full-color mark at generous scale on gallery white, title "Brand Standards" in Caslon Display, a one-line positioning statement. Right third: navy column listing the nine chapters as a catalogue index with page links. Primary action: Begin with Essence.

FORM: Category canon (standing exit), peer bar Christie's International Real Estate; seed key 51ee67f4 (degraded roll).
