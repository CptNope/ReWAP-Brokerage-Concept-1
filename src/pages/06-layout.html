6.3

Section rhythm

Pages alternate density like rooms in a gallery: a full room, then a quiet one. Never two dense sections in a row; never more than one navy statement room per page.

  • GalleryThe default room: white ground, index margin, generous space.
  • PlasterThe alternate room, for plates, tables and comparisons.
  • StatementRegistry Navy, one per page, for the single sentence the page exists to say, or the closing call to action.
  • LedgerData at full width: search results, market tables. Ends with its source line.
  • Spacing ruleMore space above a heading than below it; the page’s closing section is always anchored by a clear next step.
6.4

Editorial compositions

Five arrangements cover nearly every page. Each is asymmetric by default; centring is reserved for the single statement line.

6.5

Designed for each screen

Mobile is not a compressed desktop. Each range changes what the page prioritises, not just how wide it is.
