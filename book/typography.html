4.1

Families and why

Caslon is the typeface of the American founding documents and two centuries of law books; Public Sans was drawn for the United States government’s own web standards. Between them: permanence, law, civic clarity. Both are open-source, so the website, the WordPress theme and every vendor can use them without licensing cost.

  • HierarchySize and the change of family carry hierarchy. Bold is rare: one bold phrase per paragraph at most.
  • PunctuationTrue apostrophes and quotation marks (’ “ ”), en dashes for ranges (3–4 beds), the multiplication sign for dimensions (12 × 14 ft).
  • NumbersLining, tabular figures in data; old-style figures are not used. Thousands always take a comma.
  • NeverGradient or outlined type, letter-spaced lowercase, centred body copy, more than three sizes in one component.
