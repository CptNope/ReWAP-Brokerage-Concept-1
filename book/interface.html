7.3

Search and filters

Location first, then the two filters that matter most. Everything else lives one tap away, so the bar never becomes a form.

  • Location inputSuggests towns, neighborhoods, ZIPs, addresses and MLS numbers, grouped and labelled. Arrow keys move through suggestions; Enter selects.
  • FiltersApplied filters always show as removable chips. “More filters” opens a side panel on desktop and a full sheet on phones, with a live result count on its apply button.
  • ResultsUpdates announce politely to screen readers (“112 homes match”). Never auto-scroll the page when results change.

Accordions, disclosures and paging

Legal text is part of the design, set to be read, not hidden in grey.

What does a buyer’s agency agreement commit me to?

It defines who represents you, for how long, and how that representation is paid. We will walk through every clause with you before you sign, and you will receive a copy.

Who pays the brokerage fee?

Fees are negotiable and set out in writing. We explain the options for your situation at the first meeting.
